## Commercial use

The MIT licence covers original Genobi code only. Commercial use of a model, or of what it
generates, needs a commercial licence from that model's owner wherever its
terms say so. In particular:

- **FLUX 3 Image Klein** allows commercial use of outputs only while you and
  your affiliates earn less than US$5M a year; anything else commercial needs a
  licence from [BFL](https://bfl.ai/licensing).
- **LTX-2 and LTX-2.x** (including LoRAs trained on them, such as AKUSPACE) need
  a paid licence from Lightricks for entities with annual revenue of US$10M or
  more, and for any product that directly competes with Lightricks' own.
- **SAM 3** and **Gemma 3n** are gated and carry their owners' own terms.

The person who runs a model is the licensee. A hosted Genobi run goes through
that person's own Comfy or BFL key.

### MiniMax / FastVideo H3

FastH3 OmniRef's open weights use the [MiniMax H3 Community License](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/blob/main/LICENSE),
as linked by the [FastVideo model card](https://huggingface.co/FastVideo/FastH3-Omni-Comfy)
(checked 27 October 2026). The community grant excludes the EU, UK, Republic of
Korea and USA; the licence offers a separate licensing contact for those territories.
It also sets commercial revenue and attribution conditions. This build includes
workflow JSON and adapters, not model weights. See the
[recipe and provenance](workflows/minimax-h3-omniref.README.md).

The `minimax-h3-router` entry runs the same model hosted by MiniMax, reached
through Comfy Router (`minimax/minimax-h3`, provider `minimax`) on the
person's own Comfy key; Router re-hosts the result, usually for 12 hours,
shorter if Router falls back to MiniMax's own link. No weights
are involved, and the Comfy terms below cover the Router call. Genobi treats
the community licence's conditions on outputs as still applying to this path:
the same excluded territories, attribution and the machine-generated
disclosure. MiniMax's own API terms for the hosted model were not read for
this entry and are unverified.
